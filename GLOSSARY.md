# Fabula Ultima Reference

An unofficial, private, Portuguese-language reference for the Fabula Ultima tabletop RPG, used by Players and Game Masters at one table to find what they need to create a character and to play. Game terms shown to Readers come from the Portuguese Core Rulebook; the names below are the app's own domain language.

## Language

### Content

**Entry**:
A single self-contained reference page about one piece of the game, with a name, a Summary and a Page reference.
_Avoid_: Term, article, record, page

**Entry Type**:
The kind of an Entry: Rule, Class, Skill, Heroic Skill, Spell, Equipment, Creature, Service, Transport, or Guide.
_Avoid_: Category (a Fabula Ultima game term for weapons), kind (reserved for Equipment Kind)

**Rule**:
An Entry explaining a core play concept or a character-creation concept, such as Checks, Crisis, Fabula Points, Bonds, or Clocks, or a game-setup concept such as Session Zero or the Eight Pillars.
_Avoid_: Term, concept, mechanic

**Class**:
An Entry for one Fabula Ultima Class, showing its free benefits and, inline, all of its Skills and Spells.
_Avoid_: Job, archetype

**Skill**:
An Entry for one Skill belonging to a Class, with its maximum Skill Level. Shown to Readers as "Poder".
_Avoid_: Ability, perk; never name the type `power` in code

**Spell**:
An Entry for one spell, with its MP cost, target, duration, whether it is offensive, and the Class it belongs to. Shown to Readers as "Feitiço".
_Avoid_: Magic, incantation

**Heroic Skill**:
An Entry for one Skill a character may take on mastering a Class. It belongs to no Class and has no Skill Level; it may have Requirements, and it comes from one Source. Shown to Readers as "Poder Heroico".
_Avoid_: Heroic power, epic skill, perk; never name the type `power` in code

**Mastery**:
The state of a character who has taken the tenth level in a Class: the Class is mastered, earns the character one Heroic Skill, and cannot take further levels. Shown to Readers as "dominar uma classe".
_Avoid_: Maxing out, completing a Class (a Skill reaching its maximum Skill Level is not Mastery)

**Heroic Skill Requirement**:
What a Heroic Skill demands before it can be taken: nothing, Mastery of one specific Class, of any one of several Classes, of a number of Classes out of a set, optionally also holding a specific Skill. Shown to Readers as "requisitos".
_Avoid_: Prerequisite, unlock

**Skill Level**:
How many times a Skill has been taken, which strengthens it. Shown to Readers as "nível de poder (NP)".
_Avoid_: Rank, power level

**Equipment**:
An Entry for a weapon, armor, shield, accessory, or artifact (including a Piloto's vehicle modules).
_Avoid_: Item, gear

**Equipment Kind**:
Which of weapon, armor, shield, accessory, or artifact an Equipment Entry is.
_Avoid_: Equipment type, equipment category

**Creature**:
An Entry for one adversary of the Game Master's: a stat block with a level, a Species, Attributes, Hit Points, defenses, damage Affinities and its attacks, spells and special rules. A Villain is a Creature the Game Master declares one; it is not a separate type. Shown to Readers as "criatura".
_Avoid_: Monster ("Monstro" is a Species, not a type), enemy, adversary, NPC (the book also calls a Creature the Game Master builds an NPC)

**Creature Group**:
A Creature Entry with no stat block of its own that gathers the Creatures that fight together or belong together: the parts of one boss (a head and its tentacles) or the members of a family (such as the Ghosts). It carries the shared description, tactics and routine; each member is a Creature that points to it, and its page shows the members' stat blocks inline. Shown to Readers as "grupo de criaturas".
_Avoid_: Family (one kind of Creature Group), Party (the heroes' group)

**Custom Weapon**:
A weapon a Reader assembles from a base weapon category plus customizations, following the Atlas High Fantasy rules. It is not an Entry. Shown to Readers as "arma personalizada".
_Avoid_: Homebrew weapon, crafted weapon

**Rare Weapon**:
A weapon with a special effect beyond a basic weapon's. Either an Equipment Entry from the books or one a Reader designs in the Weapon Forge; "rare" is a rarity, not an Equipment Kind. Shown to Readers as "arma rara".
_Avoid_: Magic weapon, unique weapon

**Customization**:
One of the three options a Custom Weapon takes from the Atlas list (such as Certeira, Elemental, Mutável or Rápida); each can be taken once per weapon. Shown to Readers as "personalização". It is not a Weapon Feature.
_Avoid_: Upgrade, modifier, perk; not the Role Customization of Quick Assembly

**Weapon Feature**:
The single special property a Rare Weapon may carry, chosen from the Core Rulebook's priced list (such as Anticondição, Multi or Perfuradora) or a custom effect the Game Master prices. Shown to Readers as "característica". It is not a Customization.
_Avoid_: Trait (a Trait is Identity, Theme or Origin), property, enchantment

**Weapon Forge**:
The modal on the weapons list where a Reader builds a Custom Weapon or a Rare Weapon. Nothing built there is saved: closing the modal or reloading the page discards it. Shown to Readers as "Forja de Armas".
_Avoid_: Weapon builder, generator, creator

### Guidance

**Guide**:
An Entry that walks a Reader through an ordered list of Guide Steps toward a goal, such as "Create a world", "Create a party", "Build a character" or "How to play".
_Avoid_: Tutorial, wizard, path

**Guide Step**:
One stop in a Guide, with a title, a short introduction, and the Entries to read at that point.
_Avoid_: Chapter, stage

### Sources and writing

**Core Rulebook**:
The Brazilian edition of the Fabula Ultima core rulebook ("Livro Básico", Jambô Editora, 1st edition, November 2025), the primary source of content.
_Avoid_: Livro (alone), manual, SRD

**Atlas**:
One of the three Jambô supplement books, "Atlas High Fantasy", "Atlas Natural Fantasy" and "Atlas Techno Fantasy" (1st edition, November 2025), which add Classes, rare Equipment and artifacts. Together with the Core Rulebook they are four of the five **Sources** of an Entry (the fifth is the Bestiary Vol. I).
_Avoid_: Expansion, supplement (in the UI), setting book

**Bestiary Vol. I**:
The English-language "Fabula Ultima Bestiary, Volume I" (Need Games and Rooster Games, 2026), a fifth **Source** for Creatures. It has no Brazilian edition, so every Entry from it is the app's own translation into Portuguese: unofficial terms are marked as such, the original English name is kept and searchable, and the Page Reference is the page printed in the English book.
_Avoid_: Bestiário (the Core Rulebook's own, shorter Bestiário is a different Source), Vol. 1

**Page Reference**:
The page number printed on the page, in the Entry's Source (the Core Rulebook unless the Entry says otherwise), where its full rules appear, shown on every Entry with the book's name. It is not the PDF page index, which is 4 higher in the Core Rulebook, 2 higher in the Atlas books and 1 higher in the Bestiary Vol. I.
_Avoid_: Source, citation, footnote

**Summary**:
An Entry's short account of the rules in the app's own words, keeping rules and numbers exact and never copying the book's text.
_Avoid_: Definition, description, excerpt

### Game vocabulary

Names Readers see, taken from the Core Rulebook. The English name is the one used in code and docs.

**Trait**:
One of a character's three defining statements: Identity ("Identidade"), Theme ("Tema") and Origin ("Origem").
_Avoid_: Characteristic

**Bond**:
A connection between a character and another, with up to three emotions. Shown to Readers as "Laço".
_Avoid_: Relationship, link

**Attribute**:
One of the four core character scores, each a die size from d6 to d12: Dexterity ("Destreza", DES), Might ("Vigor", VIG), Insight ("Astúcia", AST) and Willpower ("Vontade", VON).
_Avoid_: Stat, ability score

**Hit Points / Mana Points**:
The two resource pools of a character, shown to Readers as "Pontos de Vida (PV)" and "Pontos de Mana (PM)".
_Avoid_: Health, energy

**Crisis**:
The state of having current Hit Points at or below half of the maximum, rounded down. Shown to Readers as "Crise".
_Avoid_: Bloodied, wounded

**Species**:
What kind of being a Creature is, one of eight: Construct, Demon, Elemental, Beast, Humanoid, Monster, Undead or Plant ("Construto", "Demônio", "Elemental", "Fera", "Humanoide", "Monstro", "Morto-vivo", "Planta"). It sets a few special rules (immunities, Vulnerabilities) and how many powers a built Creature starts with.
_Avoid_: Race, kind, creature type

**Inventory Points**:
The pool a character spends to pull items from the backpack during play. Shown to Readers as "Pontos de Inventário (PI)".
_Avoid_: Item slots, carry weight

**Condition**:
A temporary status, such as Abalado or Lento, that lowers one or more Attribute dice. Shown to Readers as "Condição".
_Avoid_: Status effect, debuff

**Check**:
A dice roll resolving an uncertain action. Shown to Readers as "Teste".
_Avoid_: Roll, test

**Clock**:
A circle divided into Clock Sections that tracks progress toward a goal or the approach of a danger. The Game Master creates and runs it, and everyone at the Table can see it. It is tied to a goal or danger, never to a method. Shown to Readers as "Relógio".
_Avoid_: Timer, counter

**Clock Section**:
One of the divisions of a Clock; filling or erasing Clock Sections is how a Clock moves. Shown to Readers as "seção".
_Avoid_: Slice, segment, tick

**Fabula Points**:
The meta-currency of the Players' characters. Each starts with three, gains more by facing adversity (including every time a Villain enters a scene), and spends them to invoke Traits and Bonds, to change the story, or to power certain Skills. Spending them is also a main way the Party earns experience. Shown to Readers as "Pontos de Fabula".
_Avoid_: Hero points

**Ultima Points**:
The Game Master's counterpart to Fabula Points, held by a Villain: a fixed reserve set by its Villain Tier, spent to flee, invoke a Trait or recover, and never recharged. A Villain with none left is an ordinary NPC. Shown to Readers as "Pontos de Ultima".
_Avoid_: Boss points, villain HP

**Villain**:
A powerful antagonist the Game Master declares to be one, with a Villain Tier and a reserve of Ultima Points. Its entering a scene gives every Player's character a Fabula Point. Shown to Readers as "vilão".
_Avoid_: Boss, enemy (an ordinary enemy is an NPC)

**Villain Tier**:
How important a Villain is to the story: minor, major or supreme, giving 5, 10 or 15 Ultima Points. The Game Master may keep it secret. Shown to Readers as "vilão menor / maior / supremo".
_Avoid_: Villain level, rank

**Escalation**:
The Game Master's declaration that a Villain has taken a new, darker form one Villain Tier higher (a supreme Villain cannot escalate). The Villain counts as new, so its Ultima Points reset to the new Tier's value. Shown to Readers as "escalonamento".
_Avoid_: Level up, evolution (neither a Skill Level nor a Class level is involved)

### Building Creatures

**Rank**:
How much of a fight a Creature is worth: soldier (about one Player's character of its level), elite (two soldiers) or champion (any number of soldiers it replaces; a boss is a champion). Shown to Readers as "patente" (soldado, elite, campeão).
_Avoid_: Tier (reserved for Villain Tier), class, grade

**Affinity**:
How a Creature fares against one of the nine damage types: Vulnerable, Resistant, Immune, Absorbs, or none. Shown to Readers as "Afinidade".
_Avoid_: Weakness, resistance (alone; Resistant is one Affinity)

**Study**:
The action in which a character tries to learn about someone or something; a Creature's stat block may list what a successful Study reveals. Shown to Readers as "Estudo".
_Avoid_: Scan, identify, lore check

**Quick Assembly**:
The method in the Bestiary Vol. I for building a Creature fast: pick a Role, a level and a Rank, copy that Role's profile, then add Role Skills, Boss Skills, Negative Skills and a Species's options. It is what the Creature builder follows. Shown to Readers as "Montagem Rápida" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Standard creation (the Core Rulebook's own method is not offered by the app's builder)

**Creature Assembler**:
The modal on the Creatures list where a Reader builds a Creature by following Quick Assembly. Nothing built there is saved: the Reader can only copy the finished stat block as text, and closing the modal or reloading the page discards it. Shown to Readers as "Montagem de Criaturas", opened by the button "Montar criatura".
_Avoid_: Forge (reserved for weapons), Evocation ("evocar" is the book's verb for invoking a Trait or Bond), creature builder, generator

**Role**:
One of six functions a Creature plays in a fight under Quick Assembly: brute, hunter, mage, saboteur, sentinel or support. Shown to Readers as "função" (unofficial translation), with the Roles named Bruto, Caçador, Mago, Sabotador, Sentinela and Apoio (also unofficial).
_Avoid_: Class (a Class belongs to a Player's character), archetype

**Role Skill**:
A special ability a Creature takes from the list of its Role under Quick Assembly: a special rule, an extra action, or a change to a basic attack. A level and a Rank decide how many it gets. Shown to Readers as "poder de função" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Skill (alone; a Skill belongs to a Class), NPC power

**Role Customization**:
What a soldier or elite takes under Quick Assembly in place of a Role Skill: one option from a short list of its Role, or what the Role gives instead (more Hit Points, an Immunity or one more Role Skill). A champion takes none. Shown to Readers as "personalização" (unofficial translation); it is not a Custom Weapon's Customization.
_Avoid_: Customization (alone; that is the Atlas Custom Weapon option), upgrade

**Boss Skill**:
A special ability that only a champion Creature may take under Quick Assembly, giving a boss its own gimmick. Shown to Readers as "poder de chefe" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Villain Skill, Heroic Skill (a different thing, taken by a Player's character)

**Negative Skill**:
A deliberate weakness a Creature may take under Quick Assembly in exchange for an extra Role Skill or Boss Skill. Shown to Readers as "poder negativo" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Flaw, drawback, debuff

### Travel and money

**Zenit**:
The currency of the game, written as a number followed by "z". Shown to Readers as "zênite".
_Avoid_: Gold, coin, money

**Travel**:
Moving the Party across the World's map, counted in days of Travel; a Transport multiplies how far one day takes the Party. Shown to Readers as "Viagem".
_Avoid_: Journey, trip

**Service**:
Something a Party pays Zeniths for and does not keep, such as a night's rest, a recharge of Inventory Points, or a fare for a day of Travel. Shown to Readers as "serviço".
_Avoid_: Item, purchase

**Transport**:
A Mount or a Vehicle a Party buys, which carries a number of passengers and multiplies the distance of a day of Travel. It is not Equipment. Shown to Readers as "meio de transporte" or "transporte".
_Avoid_: Item, vehicle (alone; a Vehicle is one kind of Transport)

**Mount**:
A Transport that is a creature, or a creature-like conveyance, bought in generic form. It has no statistics and cannot fight. Shown to Readers as "montaria".
_Avoid_: Pet, companion (the Andarilho's "Companheiro Fiel" is a Skill)

**Vehicle**:
A Transport that is a built craft, such as a cart, boat or aircraft, bigger than a Mount and carrying more passengers. Shown to Readers as "veículo".
_Avoid_: Car, ship

**Medium**:
Where a Transport or a Travel Service can go: land, water, underwater, air, or, in Techno Fantasy, between planets or stars. Shown to Readers as "meio" in list filters.
_Avoid_: Terrain, environment

**Cost**:
The price of a Service or Transport, in Zeniths. Shown to Readers as "custo".
_Avoid_: Price

### Setting up a game

**Session Zero**:
The first meeting of the Table, before play, where the Table settles its expectations, tone and themes, creates the World, picks a Party Type and creates the characters. Shown to Readers as "sessão zero".
_Avoid_: Setup session, prologue (the Prologue is a different thing, played in the first session)

**World**:
The shared setting the Table creates for its adventures: there is no default or canonical one. Recorded on a world sheet ("ficha de mundo") and a map sheet ("ficha de mapa"). Shown to Readers as "mundo", or "cenário".
_Avoid_: Campaign, setting book (an Atlas describes example locations, not the Table's World)

**Eight Pillars**:
The eight traits every World shares, whatever else the Table invents. Shown to Readers as "Oito Pilares".
_Avoid_: Core themes, canon

**Party**:
The group of heroes the Players create together, defined by a Party Type. It may be given a name. Shown to Readers as "grupo".
_Avoid_: Group (alone; in the book it also means the people at the Table), team, guild

**Party Type**:
One of five premises for a Party: Explorers, Guardians, Resistance Heroes, Revolutionaries, or United by Fate ("Exploradores", "Guardiões", "Heróis da Resistência", "Revolucionários", "Unidos pelo Destino"). It says which themes the first adventures focus on, with guiding questions, and works as a reference for creating heroes, not a restriction. Shown to Readers as "tipo de grupo".
_Avoid_: Archetype, party class

### People

**Reader**:
A Player or the Game Master using the app. Both roles are equally important and are always capitalized.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, customer

**Table**:
The Players and the Game Master who play together. Its agreements (frequency, tone, lines and veils) are made in Session Zero. Shown to Readers as "seu grupo" or "a mesa".
_Avoid_: Group (alone; see Party), campaign group
