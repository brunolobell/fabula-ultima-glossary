# Fabula Ultima Reference

An unofficial, private, Portuguese-language reference for the Fabula Ultima tabletop RPG, used by Players and Game Masters at one table to find what they need to create a character and to play. Game terms shown to Readers come from the Portuguese Core Rulebook; the names below are the app's own domain language.

## Language

### Content

**Entry**:
A single self-contained reference page about one piece of the game, with a name, a Summary and a Page reference.
_Avoid_: Term, article, record, page

**Entry Type**:
The kind of an Entry: Rule, Class, Skill, Heroic Skill, Peculiarity, Spell, Equipment, Creature, Service, Transport, or Guide.
_Avoid_: Category (a Fabula Ultima game term for weapons), kind (reserved for Equipment Kind)

**Rule**:
An Entry explaining a core play concept or a character-creation concept, such as Checks, Crisis, Fabula Points, Bonds, or Clocks, or a game-setup concept such as Session Zero or the Eight Pillars.
_Avoid_: Term, concept, mechanic

**Class**:
An Entry for one Fabula Ultima Class, showing its free benefits and, inline, all of its Skills and Spells.
_Avoid_: Job, archetype

**Skill**:
An Entry for one Skill belonging to a Class, with its maximum Skill Level. Shown to Readers as "Poder".
_Avoid_: Ability, perk; never name the type `power` in code

**Spell**:
An Entry for one spell, with its MP cost, target, duration, whether it is offensive, and the Class it belongs to. Shown to Readers as "Feitiço".
_Avoid_: Magic, incantation

**Heroic Skill**:
An Entry for one Skill a character may take on mastering a Class. It belongs to no Class and has no Skill Level; it may have Requirements, and it comes from one Source. Shown to Readers as "Poder Heroico".
_Avoid_: Heroic power, epic skill, perk; never name the type `power` in code

**Peculiarity**:
An Entry for one option of an optional Atlas rule that gives a Player's character a single mechanical and thematic mark of what makes it unique (a curse, a prophecy, flight, an heirloom). A character has at most one, and no two characters in the same Party share one; it may be lost and replaced when the story calls for it. It belongs to no Class, has no Skill Level and comes from one Source; a name that several Atlases print again is a single Entry, kept in the earliest Atlas, and its page says where else it appears. Shown to Readers as "peculiaridade".
_Avoid_: Quirk, flaw, Trait (a Trait is Identity, Theme or Origin), Skill, Heroic Skill

**Mastery**:
The state of a character who has taken the tenth level in a Class: the Class is mastered, earns the character one Heroic Skill, and cannot take further levels. Shown to Readers as "dominar uma classe".
_Avoid_: Maxing out, completing a Class (a Skill reaching its maximum Skill Level is not Mastery)

**Heroic Skill Requirement**:
What a Heroic Skill demands before it can be taken: nothing, Mastery of one specific Class, of any one of several Classes, of a number of Classes out of a set, optionally also holding a specific Skill. Shown to Readers as "requisitos".
_Avoid_: Prerequisite, unlock

**Skill Level**:
How many times a Skill has been taken, which strengthens it. Shown to Readers as "nível de poder (NP)".
_Avoid_: Rank, power level

**Equipment**:
An Entry for a weapon, armor, shield, accessory, or artifact (including a Piloto's vehicle modules).
_Avoid_: Item, gear

**Equipment Kind**:
Which of weapon, armor, shield, accessory, or artifact an Equipment Entry is.
_Avoid_: Equipment type, equipment category

**Creature**:
An Entry for one adversary of the Game Master's: a stat block with a level, a Species, Attributes, Hit Points, defenses, damage Affinities and its attacks, spells and special rules. A Villain is a Creature the Game Master declares one; it is not a separate type. Shown to Readers as "criatura".
_Avoid_: Monster ("Monstro" is a Species, not a type), enemy, adversary, NPC (the book also calls a Creature the Game Master builds an NPC)

**Creature Group**:
A Creature Entry with no stat block of its own that gathers the Creatures that fight together or belong together: the parts of one boss (a head and its tentacles) or the members of a family (such as the Ghosts). It carries the shared description, tactics and routine; each member is a Creature that points to it, and its page shows the members' stat blocks inline. Shown to Readers as "grupo de criaturas".
_Avoid_: Family (one kind of Creature Group), Party (the heroes' group)

**Custom Weapon**:
A weapon a Reader assembles from a base weapon category plus customizations, following the Atlas High Fantasy rules. One a Reader builds is not an Entry, but the three Atlases print basic examples (300 to 400z), and those are Equipment Entries. Shown to Readers as "arma personalizada".
_Avoid_: Homebrew weapon, crafted weapon

**Rare Weapon**:
A weapon with a special effect beyond a basic weapon's. Either an Equipment Entry from the books or one a Reader designs in the Weapon Forge; "rare" is a rarity, not an Equipment Kind. Shown to Readers as "arma rara".
_Avoid_: Magic weapon, unique weapon

**Rare Evolution**:
A Rare Weapon the Atlas prints as the "possible rare evolution" of one of its basic Custom Weapon examples, with its own price; its Entry links to that example. Shown to Readers as "evolução rara". A Rare Evolution may have two Forms (the Mutável Customization).
_Avoid_: Upgrade, variant

**Quality**:
An add-on property priced separately (for example +1.000z) that a Bestiary Vol. I Creature's reward lets a Game Master attach to a piece of gear it fits. It is not an Equipment Entry and not an Armor or Weapon Feature. Shown to Readers as "qualidade" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Feature, trait, enchantment

**Rare Armor / Rare Shield**:
An armor or shield with a special effect beyond a basic one's: a basic armor or shield from the Core Rulebook plus a single Armor Feature, and the feature's cost added to the base price. Either an Equipment Entry from the books or one a Reader designs in the Armor Forge. Shown to Readers as "armadura rara" and "escudo raro".
_Avoid_: Magic armor, unique armor

**Armor Feature**:
The single special property a Rare Armor or Rare Shield carries, chosen from the Core Rulebook's priced list (defensive ones such as Anticondição or Resistência, and "melhoria" ones such as Mais Iniciativa or Mais dano) or a custom effect the Game Master prices. The same list serves armors and shields. Shown to Readers as "característica". It is not a Weapon Feature, which has its own list.
_Avoid_: Trait (a Trait is Identity, Theme or Origin), property, enchantment

**Customization**:
One of the three options a Custom Weapon takes from the Atlas list (such as Certeira, Elemental, Mutável or Rápida); each can be taken once per weapon. Shown to Readers as "personalização". It is not a Weapon Feature.
_Avoid_: Upgrade, modifier, perk; not the Role Customization of Quick Assembly

**Weapon Feature**:
The single special property a Rare Weapon may carry, chosen from the Core Rulebook's priced list (such as Anticondição, Multi or Perfuradora) or a custom effect the Game Master prices. Shown to Readers as "característica". It is not a Customization.
_Avoid_: Trait (a Trait is Identity, Theme or Origin), property, enchantment

**Weapon Forge**:
The modal on the weapons list where a Reader builds a Custom Weapon or a Rare Weapon. Nothing built there is saved: closing the modal or reloading the page discards it. Shown to Readers as "Forja de Armas".
_Avoid_: Weapon builder, generator, creator

**Armor Forge**:
The modal on the armors and shields lists where a Reader builds a Rare Armor or a Rare Shield. Nothing built there is saved: closing the modal or reloading the page discards it. Shown to Readers as "Forja de Armaduras e Escudos".
_Avoid_: Armor builder, generator, creator

### Guidance

**Guide**:
An Entry that walks a Reader through an ordered list of Guide Steps toward a goal, such as "Create a world", "Create a party", "Build a character" or "How to play".
_Avoid_: Tutorial, wizard, path

**Guide Step**:
One stop in a Guide, with a title, a short introduction, and the Entries to read at that point.
_Avoid_: Chapter, stage

### Sources and writing

**Core Rulebook**:
The Brazilian edition of the Fabula Ultima core rulebook ("Livro Básico", Jambô Editora, 1st edition, November 2025), the primary source of content.
_Avoid_: Livro (alone), manual, SRD

**Atlas**:
One of the three Jambô supplement books, "Atlas High Fantasy", "Atlas Natural Fantasy" and "Atlas Techno Fantasy" (1st edition, November 2025), which add Classes, rare Equipment and artifacts. Together with the Core Rulebook they are four of the eight **Sources** of an Entry (the others are the Bestiary Vol. I, the Codex Extra and the two Fabula Bonus PDFs).
_Avoid_: Expansion, supplement (in the UI), setting book

**Bestiary Vol. I**:
The English-language "Fabula Ultima Bestiary, Volume I" (Need Games and Rooster Games, 2026), the translated **Source** for Creatures. It has no Brazilian edition, so every Entry from it is the app's own translation into Portuguese: unofficial terms are marked as such, the original English name is kept and searchable, and the Page Reference is the page printed in the English book.
_Avoid_: Bestiário (the Core Rulebook's own, shorter Bestiário is a different Source), Vol. 1

**Codex Extra**:
The Brazilian "Codex Extra" (Jambô Editora, 2025), an official **Source** that adds six Classes (Astrólogo, Bandoleiro, Cavaleiro Mágico, Forjador de Almas, Lâmina Carmesim, Malandro) and a list of new Heroic Skills. Its Arcanos, Peculiaridades and "Novos Personagens Clássicos" are not in the app. Its own printed Carteador and Necromante are not used either: the Fabula Bonus versions are.
_Avoid_: Codex (alone), Livro Extra

**Fabula Bonus**:
Two free English PDFs, "Fabula Ultima Bonus: Ace of Cards" and "Fabula Ultima Bonus 01: Necromancer" (Need Games), each a **Source** (`bonus-ace-of-cards`, `bonus-necromancer`) because both have a page 3. They give the Classes Carteador and Necromante and 7 Heroic Skills. There is no official Brazilian edition of them, so every Entry is the app's own translation, reusing the Codex Extra's Portuguese names; the original English name is kept and searchable, the label ends in "(inglês)", and the Page Reference is the page printed in the English PDF.
_Avoid_: Bonus (alone), Ace of Cards and Necromancer as Class names (the Classes are "Carteador" and "Necromante")

**Page Reference**:
The page number printed on the page, in the Entry's Source (the Core Rulebook unless the Entry says otherwise), where its full rules appear, shown on every Entry with the book's name. It is not the PDF page index, which is 4 higher in the Core Rulebook, 2 higher in the Atlas books and the Codex Extra, 1 higher in the Bestiary Vol. I; the two Fabula Bonus PDFs have no offset.
_Avoid_: Source, citation, footnote

**Summary**:
An Entry's short account of the rules in the app's own words, keeping rules and numbers exact and never copying the book's text.
_Avoid_: Definition, description, excerpt

### Game vocabulary

Names Readers see, taken from the Core Rulebook. The English name is the one used in code and docs.

**Trait**:
One of a character's three defining statements: Identity ("Identidade"), Theme ("Tema") and Origin ("Origem").
_Avoid_: Characteristic

**Bond**:
A connection between a character and another, with up to three emotions. Shown to Readers as "Laço".
_Avoid_: Relationship, link

**Attribute**:
One of the four core character scores, each a die size from d6 to d12: Dexterity ("Destreza", DES), Might ("Vigor", VIG), Insight ("Astúcia", AST) and Willpower ("Vontade", VON).
_Avoid_: Stat, ability score

**Hit Points / Mana Points**:
The two resource pools of a character, shown to Readers as "Pontos de Vida (PV)" and "Pontos de Mana (PM)".
_Avoid_: Health, energy

**Crisis**:
The state of having current Hit Points at or below half of the maximum, rounded down. Shown to Readers as "Crise".
_Avoid_: Bloodied, wounded

**Species**:
What kind of being a Creature is, one of eight: Construct, Demon, Elemental, Beast, Humanoid, Monster, Undead or Plant ("Construto", "Demônio", "Elemental", "Fera", "Humanoide", "Monstro", "Morto-vivo", "Planta"). It sets a few special rules (immunities, Vulnerabilities) and how many powers a built Creature starts with. Quick Assembly has its own Species rules (Bestiary Vol. I), which replace the Core Rulebook's for a Creature built with it.
_Avoid_: Race, kind, creature type

**Inventory Points**:
The pool a character spends to pull items from the backpack during play. Shown to Readers as "Pontos de Inventário (PI)".
_Avoid_: Item slots, carry weight

**Magic Tent**:
An item a character creates by spending 4 Inventory Points; it is big enough for the whole Party and lets it Rest in the wilderness. Shown to Readers as "barraca mágica".
_Avoid_: Camp, shelter (a safe place is another way to Rest)

**Condition**:
A temporary status, such as Abalado or Lento, that lowers one or more Attribute dice. Shown to Readers as "Condição".
_Avoid_: Status effect, debuff

**Rest**:
Hours of inactivity (four to six) in a safe place, after which a Player's character recovers all Hit Points and Mana Points and all Conditions. The Party needs a Magic Tent, a friendly host or a safe place in the wilderness, or an inn or a host in a settlement. NPCs do not use the rule. Shown to Readers as "descanso".
_Avoid_: Sleep, long rest, short rest (the game has one kind of Rest)

**Rest Scene**:
The quiet, in-character scene played during a Rest, at the end of which each Player's character may create a Bond, add an emotion to one or replace one. Shown to Readers as "cena de descanso".
_Avoid_: Downtime, interlude (an Interlude is a different kind of scene)

**Camp Activity**:
An optional Natural Fantasy rule: each Player's character has two Camp Activities and may do one per Rest Scene, giving a benefit that lasts until the next Rest. Shown to Readers as "atividade de acampamento".
_Avoid_: Camp action, downtime activity

**Check**:
A dice roll resolving an uncertain action. Shown to Readers as "Teste".
_Avoid_: Roll, test

**Difficulty Level**:
The number a Check's result must equal or beat for the action to succeed, set by a rule or chosen by the Game Master (7 easy, 10 normal, 13 hard, 16 very hard; 10 when in doubt). Shown to Readers as "nível de dificuldade (ND)". A Check with no Difficulty Level is an Open Check or an Opposed Check.
_Avoid_: DC, target number

**High Roll**:
The larger of the two dice of a Check, used to work out effects such as weapon and spell damage. Shown to Readers as "rolagem alta (RA)".
_Avoid_: Highest die, best roll

**Critical Success / Critical Failure**:
A Critical Success is both dice showing the same number, 6 or higher; it always succeeds. A Critical Failure is both dice showing 1; it always fails, and a player character who rolls one gains a Fabula Point. Both give an Opportunity. Shown to Readers as "sucesso crítico" and "falha crítica".
_Avoid_: Crit, natural 20, fumble

**Opportunity**:
A twist granted by a Critical Success (to the roller) or a Critical Failure (to whoever controls the opposition), spent on one of eleven listed options or an invented one that fits the scene. Shown to Readers as "oportunidade".
_Avoid_: Bonus, boon, reward

**Opposed Check**:
A Check made by each side of a contest with the same Attributes and no Difficulty Level; the higher result wins and a tie is rerolled. Shown to Readers as "teste oposto".
_Avoid_: Contested roll

**Open Check**:
A Check with no Difficulty Level, where the result says how well the character did (7+, 10+, 13+ or 16+). Shown to Readers as "teste aberto".
_Avoid_: Knowledge check

**Group Check**:
A Check in which player characters cooperate: a leader makes the final Check and each supporter who passes a Support Check adds +1, plus the strongest Bond a supporter has with the leader. Shown to Readers as "teste em grupo".
_Avoid_: Team roll, help action

**Invoke**:
To spend one Fabula Point to call on a Trait (reroll one or both dice) or a Bond (add its strength) during a Check. Shown to Readers as "evocar".
_Avoid_: Use, activate, call

**Action**:
What a character does with its turn in a conflict: one of ten kinds (Attack, Skill, Spell, Study, Guard, Hinder, Objective, Inventory, Equipment, or Other), chosen as the focus of the turn. Shown to Readers as "ação". The Equipment action ("ação Equipamento") only swaps what a character has equipped; it is not Equipment.
_Avoid_: Move, maneuver, turn (the turn is when a character acts; the Action is what it does)

**Minor Action**:
A small thing a character may do in its turn alongside its Action: picking up or dropping an item, throwing one to another character, or a brief interaction with the surroundings. The Game Master has the final word on what counts, and anything that needs a Check must be the turn's Action. Shown to Readers as "ação menor".
_Avoid_: Free action (a "free attack" is a different thing: an Attack made as part of another effect), bonus action

**Guard**:
The Action in which a character braces to protect its allies: until the start of its next turn it is Resistant to every damage type, gains +2 on opposed Checks, and may give cover to another creature. It can be done only once per turn and its benefits never stack. Shown to Readers as "Guarda".
_Avoid_: Defend, dodge, block, parry

**Hinder**:
The Action in which a character puts an opponent at a disadvantage (a feint, a distraction, a cutting remark) with a Check against a fixed difficulty of 10; on a success the opponent suffers one Condition of the character's choice from a short list. Shown to Readers as "Impedimento".
_Avoid_: Debuff, taunt, disrupt

**Objective**:
The Action in which a character works toward a goal of the conflict, nearly always by moving a Clock with a Check. Shown to Readers as "Objetivo". It is the Action, not the conflict's goal itself and not the Clock that tracks it.
_Avoid_: Goal, quest, mission (those name what is being pursued, not the Action)

**Clock**:
A circle divided into Clock Sections that tracks progress toward a goal or the approach of a danger. The Game Master creates and runs it, and everyone at the Table can see it. It is tied to a goal or danger, never to a method. Shown to Readers as "Relógio".
_Avoid_: Timer, counter

**Clock Section**:
One of the divisions of a Clock; filling or erasing Clock Sections is how a Clock moves. Shown to Readers as "seção".
_Avoid_: Slice, segment, tick

**Fabula Points**:
The meta-currency of the Players' characters. Each starts with three, gains more by facing adversity (including every time a Villain enters a scene), and spends them to invoke Traits and Bonds, to change the story, or to power certain Skills. Spending them is also a main way the Party earns experience. Shown to Readers as "Pontos de Fabula".
_Avoid_: Hero points

**Ultima Points**:
The Game Master's counterpart to Fabula Points, held by a Villain: a fixed reserve set by its Villain Tier, spent to flee, invoke a Trait or recover, and never recharged. A Villain with none left is an ordinary NPC. Shown to Readers as "Pontos de Ultima".
_Avoid_: Boss points, villain HP

**Villain**:
A powerful antagonist the Game Master declares to be one, with a Villain Tier and a reserve of Ultima Points. Its entering a scene gives every Player's character a Fabula Point. Shown to Readers as "vilão".
_Avoid_: Boss, enemy (an ordinary enemy is an NPC)

**Villain Tier**:
How important a Villain is to the story: minor, major or supreme, giving 5, 10 or 15 Ultima Points. The Game Master may keep it secret. Shown to Readers as "vilão menor / maior / supremo".
_Avoid_: Villain level, rank

**Escalation**:
The Game Master's declaration that a Villain has taken a new, darker form one Villain Tier higher (a supreme Villain cannot escalate). The Villain counts as new, so its Ultima Points reset to the new Tier's value. Shown to Readers as "escalonamento".
_Avoid_: Level up, evolution (neither a Skill Level nor a Class level is involved)

### Building Creatures

**Rank**:
How much of a fight a Creature is worth: soldier (about one Player's character of its level), elite (two soldiers) or champion (any number of soldiers it replaces; a boss is a champion). Shown to Readers as "patente" (soldado, elite, campeão).
_Avoid_: Tier (reserved for Villain Tier), class, grade

**Affinity**:
How a Creature fares against one of the nine damage types: Vulnerable, Resistant, Immune, Absorbs, or none. Shown to Readers as "Afinidade".
_Avoid_: Weakness, resistance (alone; Resistant is one Affinity)

**Study**:
The Action in which a character tries to learn about someone or something with an open Check, the result deciding how much it learns; a Creature's stat block may list what a successful Study reveals. Shown to Readers as "Estudo".
_Avoid_: Scan, identify, lore check

**Quick Assembly**:
The method in the Bestiary Vol. I for building a Creature fast: pick a Role, a level and a Rank, copy that Role's profile, then add Role Skills, Boss Skills, Negative Skills and a Species's options. It is what the Creature builder follows. Shown to Readers as "Montagem Rápida" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Standard creation (the Core Rulebook's own method is not offered by the app's builder)

**Creature Assembler**:
The page where a Reader builds a Creature by following Quick Assembly. Nothing built there is saved: the Reader can only copy the finished stat block as text, and leaving or reloading the page discards it. Shown to Readers as "Laboratório Oroboros" (the button, the menu link and the page title share the name).
_Avoid_: Forge (reserved for weapons), Evocation ("evocar" is the book's verb for invoking a Trait or Bond), creature builder, generator, "Montagem de Criaturas" and "Montar criatura" (the earlier names)

**Role**:
One of six functions a Creature plays in a fight under Quick Assembly: brute, hunter, mage, saboteur, sentinel or support. Shown to Readers as "função" (unofficial translation), with the Roles named Bruto, Caçador, Mago, Sabotador, Sentinela and Apoio (also unofficial).
_Avoid_: Class (a Class belongs to a Player's character), archetype

**NPC Spell**:
One of the 15 spells of the Core Rulebook's list for Creatures (Sopro, Roubo de Vida, Condição em Área…). The Game Master renames it and picks its damage type or condition when making the stat block. Shown to Readers as "feitiço de NPC".
_Avoid_: Class spell (a Spell belongs to a Class; a Creature may also learn some of those), monster spell

**Role Skill**:
A special ability a Creature takes from the list of its Role under Quick Assembly: a special rule, an extra action, or a change to a basic attack. A level and a Rank decide how many it gets. Shown to Readers as "poder de função" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Skill (alone; a Skill belongs to a Class), NPC power

**Role Customization**:
What a soldier or elite takes under Quick Assembly in place of a Role Skill: one option from a short list of its Role, or what the Role gives instead (more Hit Points, an Immunity or one more Role Skill). A champion takes none. Shown to Readers as "personalização" (unofficial translation); it is not a Custom Weapon's Customization.
_Avoid_: Customization (alone; that is the Atlas Custom Weapon option), upgrade

**Boss Skill**:
A special ability that only a champion Creature may take under Quick Assembly, giving a boss its own gimmick. Shown to Readers as "poder de chefe" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Villain Skill, Heroic Skill (a different thing, taken by a Player's character)

**Negative Skill**:
A deliberate weakness a Creature may take under Quick Assembly in exchange for an extra Role Skill or Boss Skill. Shown to Readers as "poder negativo" (unofficial translation).
_Avoid_: Flaw, drawback, debuff

### Travel and money

**Zenit**:
The currency of the game, written as a number followed by "z". Shown to Readers as "zênite".
_Avoid_: Gold, coin, money

**Travel**:
Moving the Party across the World's map, counted in days of Travel; a Transport multiplies how far one day takes the Party. Shown to Readers as "Viagem". The Rule that explains it keeps the book's own title, "Jornadas e viagens"; the term stays Travel.
_Avoid_: Journey, trip (as the term; "Jornadas e viagens" is only that Rule's title)

**Travel Check**:
The roll the Game Master makes for each day of Travel, with a die set by the Threat Level: 6 or more is a Danger, a 1 is a Discovery. Shown to Readers as "teste de Viagem".
_Avoid_: Travel roll, journey check

**Threat Level**:
How hostile the area crossed in a day of Travel is, from minimal to very high; it sets the die of the Travel Check (d6 to d20). When a day crosses several levels only the highest counts. Shown to Readers as "nível de ameaça".
_Avoid_: Danger level, difficulty

**Danger**:
A problem the Party meets on a day of Travel (a storm, a toxic swamp, a territorial monster, a rival) that it must avoid with a group check, a series of checks or, rarely, a conflict; it deals weak, strong or intense damage by level. Shown to Readers as "perigo".
_Avoid_: Hazard, encounter, trap

**Discovery**:
A good thing the Party finds on a day of Travel (a hidden ruin, a caravan, a refuge, a precious item or piece of information); it is always worth the trouble. Shown to Readers as "descoberta".
_Avoid_: Find, loot, reward

**Service**:
Something a Party pays Zeniths for and does not keep, such as a night's rest, a recharge of Inventory Points, or a fare for a day of Travel. Shown to Readers as "serviço".
_Avoid_: Item, purchase

**Transport**:
A Mount or a Vehicle a Party buys, which carries a number of passengers and multiplies the distance of a day of Travel. It is not Equipment. Shown to Readers as "meio de transporte" or "transporte".
_Avoid_: Item, vehicle (alone; a Vehicle is one kind of Transport)

**Mount**:
A Transport that is a creature, or a creature-like conveyance, bought in generic form. It has no statistics and cannot fight. Shown to Readers as "montaria".
_Avoid_: Pet, companion (the Andarilho's "Companheiro Fiel" is a Skill)

**Vehicle**:
A Transport that is a built craft, such as a cart, boat or aircraft, bigger than a Mount and carrying more passengers. Shown to Readers as "veículo".
_Avoid_: Car, ship

**Group Vehicle**:
An optional Techno Fantasy rule: a medium or large Transport the Party owns that fights as a single construct, built together by the players and the Game Master, with the crew taking turns through it. Shown to Readers as "veículo de grupo".
_Avoid_: Party ship, mech (a Mecha is a Piloto's personal vehicle)

**Medium**:
Where a Transport or a Travel Service can go: land, water, underwater, air, or, in Techno Fantasy, between planets or stars. Shown to Readers as "meio" in list filters.
_Avoid_: Terrain, environment

**Cost**:
The price of a Service or Transport, in Zeniths. Shown to Readers as "custo".
_Avoid_: Price

### Setting up a game

**Session Zero**:
The first meeting of the Table, before play, where the Table settles its expectations, tone and themes, creates the World, picks a Party Type and creates the characters. Shown to Readers as "sessão zero".
_Avoid_: Setup session, prologue (the Prologue is a different thing, played in the first session)

**World**:
The shared setting the Table creates for its adventures: there is no default or canonical one. Recorded on a world sheet ("ficha de mundo") and a map sheet ("ficha de mapa"). Shown to Readers as "mundo", or "cenário".
_Avoid_: Campaign, setting book (an Atlas describes example locations, not the Table's World)

**Eight Pillars**:
The eight traits every World shares, whatever else the Table invents. Shown to Readers as "Oito Pilares".
_Avoid_: Core themes, canon

**Party**:
The group of heroes the Players create together, defined by a Party Type. It may be given a name. Shown to Readers as "grupo".
_Avoid_: Group (alone; in the book it also means the people at the Table), team, guild

**Party Type**:
One of five premises for a Party: Explorers, Guardians, Resistance Heroes, Revolutionaries, or United by Fate ("Exploradores", "Guardiões", "Heróis da Resistência", "Revolucionários", "Unidos pelo Destino"). It says which themes the first adventures focus on, with guiding questions, and works as a reference for creating heroes, not a restriction. Shown to Readers as "tipo de grupo".
_Avoid_: Archetype, party class

### People

**Reader**:
A Player or the Game Master using the app. Both roles are equally important and are always capitalized.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, customer

**Table**:
The Players and the Game Master who play together. Its agreements (frequency, tone, lines and veils) are made in Session Zero. Shown to Readers as "seu grupo" or "a mesa".
_Avoid_: Group (alone; see Party), campaign group
