# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Jogadores and the Mestre of one Fabula Ultima table, Portuguese speakers (PT-BR). They open the site mid-session, often on a phone, to settle a rule or to build a character: look up a Poder, a Feitiço, a Classe, a piece of equipment. Players and Mestre are equal in importance. Most visits are short and goal-driven ("what does this Skill do at NP 3?"); a smaller share are longer browsing sessions during character creation.

## Product Purpose

Códice do Cristal is a private, unofficial, fan-made PT-BR reference for the Fabula Ultima tabletop RPG, in the spirit of Archives of Nethys: search first, then browse by type. It carries own-words summaries with exact rules and numbers, each with a printed page reference to the Livro Básico or an Atlas supplement. Success is a table that stops flipping through the book: a rule is found in a few seconds and understood without leaving the page.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, calm, honest. A well-kept codex, not a loud game portal. It says plainly when a rule may cause confusion. Fantasy flavor comes from restrained ornament (a crystal bullet, gradient rules), never from heavy theming.

## Anti-references

- The official Fabula Ultima trade dress as a theme: a green palette, filigree or flourish borders, corner ornaments, the logotype and cover layout. The product must not look official. One deliberate exception, approved by the owner for a private group: the Feather sprite and a deep teal taken from it as the primary hue, with apricot as the accent (DESIGN.md §2 and §3). It stays a blue-leaning teal, never the official logo's emerald.
- Boxed callouts and card grids; highlights are gradients without contours.
- Dense, cluttered wiki chrome and walls of unstructured text.
- Generic fantasy-RPG kitsch (parchment textures, faux-medieval fonts everywhere).

## Design Principles

1. **Find first.** Search and type browsing are the front door; every screen is one tap from either.
2. **Self-contained pages.** A reader never has to leave an Entry to understand it; related facts are repeated, not only linked.
3. **Phone at the table.** Designed at 360 px first, one column, 44 px targets, nothing that needs hover.
4. **Quiet ornament, loud clarity.** Two hues (feather teal and apricot), gradients as the only decoration (the Feather sprite is the one exception), bold for core concepts; rules text always wins over style.
5. **Honest and traceable.** Every Entry says where it comes from (book and printed page); the site never pretends to be official, and the footer says it is an independent system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA for text in light and dark themes (measured in DESIGN.md §3.4), visible 2 px focus ring on every interactive element, full keyboard operation including the search combobox, color never the only carrier of meaning, transitions limited and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`, `lang="pt-BR"`. Neutral, gender-inclusive wording in copy.
